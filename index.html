About Us

Your local butcher,
done the right way

At Cloudy Peaks Butchery, great meat is what we do. We source quality New Zealand beef, lamb, pork, and poultry and prepare every cut fresh in-store with the kind of care and skill that only a true local butcher can offer.

Whether you're planning a Sunday roast, stocking up the freezer, or hunting the perfect steak for the weekend BBQ, we'll help you find exactly what you need — and make sure it's worth coming back for.

What We Offer

Premium cuts for
every occasion

From everyday dinners to big weekend feeds, we stock the freshest, finest meat Timaru has to offer.

🥩

Beef & Steak

Eye fillet, scotch, sirloin, rump, blade — we cut to order. Premium NZ beef sourced from trusted farms and prepared the way you want it.

🐑

Lamb & Hogget

New Zealand lamb at its finest. Legs, shoulders, racks, chops — tender, flavoursome cuts straight from local farms to your plate.

🐖

Pork

Fresh pork belly, loin, ribs, and roasting joints. Perfect for the slow cooker, weekend roast, or the backyard smoker.

🍗

Chicken & Poultry

Fresh free-range chicken in every cut — whole birds, thighs, breasts, mince, and more. Always fresh, never frozen.

🌭

Sausages & Smallgoods

Made in-store using traditional recipes. Our handcrafted sausages are a local favourite and a Cloudy Peaks staple — try them once and you'll keep coming back.

📦

Bulk & Freezer Packs

Stock up and save. We offer great value bulk packs of beef, lamb, and pork — perfect for families and serious home cooks who know what they want.
