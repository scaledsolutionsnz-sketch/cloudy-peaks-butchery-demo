Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 September 2026

Cloudy Peaks Butchery respects your privacy. This page explains what happens to any information you give us through this website.

What we collect

This website does not run contact forms, accounts or online ordering. It collects no personal information from you directly, and sets no tracking or advertising cookies.

If you call or email us using the details on this site, we hold what you tell us only for as long as it takes to answer you or fill your order.

Third-party content

Two things on this site are loaded from other companies, and each has its own privacy policy:

  • Google Maps — the map on the Visit section is embedded from Google, which may record your IP address and set its own cookies when it loads.
  • Google Fonts — the typefaces are served by Google.

Your information

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask what personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Ring the shop on 027 232 2158 and we will sort it out.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
